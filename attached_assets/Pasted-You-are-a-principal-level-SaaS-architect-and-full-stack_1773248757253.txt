==================================================
IRON METRICS INTELLIGENCE LAYER
==================================================

Preserve and fully build the Iron Metrics core intelligence concepts:
- Retention Stability Index (RSI)
- Member Risk Radar
- churn probability scoring
- cohort survival analysis
- revenue scenario modeling
- intervention ranking
- lifetime value engine
- owner strategic brief
- AI operator task generation

Implement the following intelligence features:

1. RETENTION STABILITY INDEX (RSI)
Build a weighted score from:
- churn rate
- average revenue per member
- net member growth
- average member tenure
Normalize each metric to 0-100.
Display:
- current score
- band (Strong / Moderate / Fragile)
- 30 / 90 day trend
- metric contribution breakdown
- why the score moved

2. MEMBER RISK RADAR
For each active member, compute churn risk using signals like:
- attendance decay
- days since last visit
- membership age / tenure bucket
- billing issues
- contact recency
- class variety / engagement
- prior holds / cancellation history
- onboarding completion
- milestone participation
Classify members into engagement or risk tiers.
Sort by projected revenue risk.

3. COHORT ANALYSIS
Group members by join month and track:
- retained at 30 / 60 / 90 / 180 / 365 days
- cancellation hotspots
- cohort revenue contribution
- onboarding weakness signals

4. REVENUE SCENARIO MODELING
Project:
- expected
- upside
- downside
for revenue over the next 3, 6, and 12 months.
Base on:
- historical churn
- acquisition trends
- revenue per member
- billing failures
- retention improvements

5. INTERVENTION ENGINE
Score recommendations using some variant of:
revenue impact × confidence × urgency
Return the top 3 recommendations across different categories.
Examples:
- onboarding
- at-risk member outreach
- billing recovery
- lead follow-up
- pricing action
- coach development
- retention campaign
Each recommendation must include:
- plain-English explanation
- why it matters
- expected impact
- action checklist
- status tracking

6. LIFETIME VALUE ENGINE
Estimate member LTV and projected LTV by segment.
Show:
- current average LTV
- LTV by membership type
- LTV impact of reducing churn
- LTV loss tied to at-risk members

7. OWNER STRATEGIC BRIEF
Generate a concise weekly and monthly executive view:
- what changed
- biggest risks
- biggest wins
- where money is leaking
- what to do next
This should feel like an operator briefing, not a generic report.
